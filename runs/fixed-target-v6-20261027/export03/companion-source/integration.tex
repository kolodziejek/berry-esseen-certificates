\section{Stronger bounds and the remaining numerical gap}

The two-pass route above is sufficient for the initial support geometry.
There are two bounded refinements. They give stronger quantitative
information but are not needed to justify that route.

First, keep the full phase correction in \eqref{v6:identity}. The
appendix gives a uniform upper bound for all real $n\ge42$ by maximizing
a degree-six polynomial majorant at the floor index. A short concavity test
leaves one critical point, enclosed by rational bisection. Combined with
the same moment coefficients, a single pass gives
\[
 T\le13.2818901598,\qquad U\le10.7965952605,\qquad
 D\le3.8675294672.
\]
Every cell already has $D<(\sigma-1)\rho$; the smallest rationally
certified cell margin is greater than $0.03589975$.
Six further circle/density updates give $D\le3.3579256801$.
The selected quadratic must itself satisfy its strict raw gate before
the exponential estimate is used. A better bound obtained elsewhere by
taking a minimum does not certify that condition for this quadratic.

Second, the actual central gap supplies a better symmetrization event.
For independent $X,X'$, use their opposite-sign event with probability
at least $1/(2u^2)$, conditional second moment at most $4u^2$, and the
proved distance bound in the preceding section. Re-evaluate the
\emph{complete} retained high-frequency bound, including all six pieces.
Keep the smaller of this valid bound and the original universal bound.
The lower-frequency estimate remains unchanged. Six bounded updates on
the same 24 cells give
\[
 T\le9.3684595861,\qquad U\le8.3681296416,\qquad
 D\le2.9976105609,\qquad nD\le125.8996435547.
\]
Sixteen cells satisfy $D<2$; eight do not. All 24 eventually improve
their high-frequency bound. None is omitted. The gap is established
\emph{before} its event is used, so this step does not assume its own
conclusion.

\paragraph{Acceptance and its limits.}
The initial cells are
$[1+3361i/96000,1+3361(i+1)/96000]$, $0\le i<24$.
All include their endpoints. The checkers freshly reconstruct the
first-cover constants from pinned source copies and use exact fractions,
directed square roots and explicit continuous-domain maxima. They
record both endpoints of the enclosure of $c$, complete high-frequency
terms and the ordered previous-defect/new-raw/new-resource transitions.
Separate checks reject missing, duplicated or reordered cells, invalid
sign/moment gates, reversed root rounding, corrupted tables and an
unlicensed phase envelope. Actual-law controls include Bernoulli,
three- and four-point laws, zero atoms and tiny distant atoms; the last
are rejected when they lie outside the acquired support domain.

Independent reviews rederive the moment and phase arguments. The
combined polynomial review reconstructs the coefficients and certifies
concavity by an independent Bernstein calculation. These checks do not
independently prove the retained Fourier primitives or validate their
entire dependency graph. A fresh arithmetic success proves only the
specified scalar implication under those premises.

The retained initial acceptor asks for much more: its final targets
include $D<0.784$, raw remainder below $2.24$, and further probability
panels. Neither the two-pass result nor the stronger refinements above
establish that contract. The final signed support/noise domains,
confinement of both deleted sums, strict count exclusion, zero-noise
binomial comparison and independent finite base remain necessary.
No existing certificate, driver or target has been removed or weakened.
