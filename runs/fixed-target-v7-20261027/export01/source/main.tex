\begin{abstract}
We complete the coarse acquisition in the fixed-target proof route.
The main improvement feeds a lower bound for the common first moment
back into the signed second moment, keeping both constraints attached
to one law. Two coarse quadratic passes and four signed passes meet
all five numerical targets of the earlier initialization.
We independently prove the complete Fourier estimate used by the
calculation and supply a distinct exact acceptance contract.
A separate harmonic improvement allows conditional span below one;
an explicit family of actual iid laws explains why small moment
resources cannot replace confinement. The finite base and a complete
confinement and count comparison remain necessary. This is not a
proof of the sharp conjecture.
\end{abstract}

\section{What has been completed}
Let $c=C_{\rm E}=(3+\sqrt{10})/(6\sqrt{2\pi})$. For a centered,
variance-one marginal, write $\rho=\E|X|^3$ and consider the excess
\[
 a=\sqrt n\{\Pp(S_n\le H)-\Phi(H/\sqrt n)\}-c\rho .
\]
The preceding fixed-target argument gives an attained witness, a
closed-support contact inequality, and a conserved identity.
At a least failing index $n\ge42$, sharp predecessors and the external
structural estimate give $1\le\rho\le7361/4000$ and $|X|\le96/25$.
The earlier two quadratic passes obtain an actual central support gap.
These are explicit entry inputs to the present calculation.

The next step used to leave too much freedom in the signed moments.
Writing $m=\E X_+$, the defect gives a lower bound for $m$, while
$4m^2+\beta^2\le1$ turns it into an upper bound for $|\beta|$.
Applying both before evaluating the resource eliminates the high-rho
boxes. Four signed passes suffice. The selected calculation uses 11264
signed boxes and exports all 100 closed probability panels; 30 are
nonempty. Exact acceptance also rejects 19 malformed or understated
proof objects.

The degree-six phase envelope, six intervening coarse HIGH updates and
an unknown extremal-score cap are absent from this selected route.
The fixed target $c$ is the coefficient throughout. The resulting
component has its own name and hypotheses; its output has not been
relabelled as an older certificate with an actual-score premise.

For orientation, the accepted coarse bounds are
\begin{gather*}
 R_c\le2.1995174,\qquad nD\le32.20665378,\\
 \rho\le1.490145834,\qquad|\beta|\le .50549963,\qquad |H|\le1.83605680 .
\end{gather*}
The original coarse contract specifies 239616 signed-box evaluations.
The smaller count here concerns that component only, with its small
quadratic ancestor counted separately. It is not a runtime comparison
or a percentage reduction of the complete proof.

The proof below first establishes the complete Fourier estimate, then
explains the signed update and its numerical contract. The final part
identifies what a strict count comparison still needs. In particular,
the finite prefix through41 is independent of these large-index
estimates. The accompanying finite-base audit records its unresolved
provider and analytic edges.

\section{Evidence, scope and authorship}
The exact source, proof object, fresh acceptance streams, input hashes
and independent review reports accompany this note. The acceptance
contract replays the two-quadratic ancestor, the support-radius gates,
all selected signed boxes and the complete probability export.
Independent reviews separately reconstruct the Fourier arguments,
the moment and gap bounds, the fixed-target transport and the panel
accounting. They are not all independent implementations of the
same numerical program.

The named Fourier lemmas are proved above under their explicit
moment and actual-event hypotheses. Their proof does not establish
those hypotheses for an arbitrary law. Likewise the new coarse
entry does not establish every subsequent confinement or count
predicate. The original certificates and theorem targets remain
unchanged. A complete finite base and ordered final exclusion must
still be supplied before an unconditional sharp theorem is claimed.

AI systems were used to develop candidate arguments, write and
revise the exposition, implement exact checks, and conduct separate
adversarial reviews. The authors and readers must assess the
displayed arguments and reproducible evidence; agreement between
AI reviews is not a proof.
