\section{A whole-rectangle resource from actual moment cells}
For each retained moment cell, write its proved scalar raw bound as
\begin{equation}\label{pc:raw}
 \mathcal R_c\le K_F+cA+H(cK_1+\varphi W)+s|H|-q(c)H^2,
 \qquad \varphi=\phi(H/\sqrt n).
\end{equation}
The cell retains ranges for $\rho,\beta,\gamma,m,p$ and the signed
quantities $K_1,W$, with the same-law density license
$\varphi\in[\varphi_-,2/5]$. Its separately proved raw ceiling is
$T<Nc_-\rho_-$; put
\[
 \eta=(c_-\rho_- -T/N)/2>0.
\]
The fixed-target phase-price identity then gives
\begin{equation}\label{pc:resource}
 \mathcal U:=(n+H^2)\mathfrak a+n\phi(0)D/6+n^{3/2}L_{\rm leak}
 \le \mathcal R_c-\eta H^2.
\end{equation}
All quantities in these inequalities refer to one law and raw threshold.
In particular, $\mathcal U$ is a joint resource, not just a defect bound.

For each endpoint $t\in\{c_-,c_+\}$, define
\[
 B_t=K_F+tA,\quad Q_t=q(t)+\eta,
\]
\[
 F_{t,+}=\max(tK_1+\varphi W)+s,\qquad
 F_{t,-}=-\min(tK_1+\varphi W)+s,
\]
where maxima and minima range over all eight corners of the recorded
$K_1,\varphi,W$ rectangle. Their multiaffine form makes the corner
evaluation valid on the full rectangle. For fixed $|H|$, maximizing
affine functions of $c$ is convex, so the two endpoints bound every
$c\in[c_-,c_+]$. Also $Q_t>0$ is explicitly checked.
Thus, for each sign $\epsilon$ of $H$,
\begin{equation}\label{pc:cell}
 \mathcal U\le
 \min\left\{T,\max_{t\in\{c_-,c_+\}}
             (B_t+F_{t,\epsilon}|H|-Q_tH^2)\right\}.
\end{equation}
The first entry $T$ is a valid, possibly loose, upper bound because
$\mathcal U\le\mathcal R_c$.

\begin{lemma}[Necessary noise filtering]\label{pc:noise}
Let $v=p(1-p)\in[v_-,v_+]$ and $0\le\lambda\le\lambda_+$.
Every actual law at every $n\ge N$ satisfies
\[
 \theta\le\Theta_\lambda:=\frac{\lambda_+}{Nv_-+\lambda_+},\qquad
 \sqrt{\frac{v_-^2}{v_-+\lambda_+/N}}\le m\le\sqrt{v_+},
\]
\[
 1-2h-2(1-l)\Theta_\lambda\le\beta
       \le1-2l+2h\Theta_\lambda.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact identities are
\[
 \lambda=\frac{nv\theta}{1-\theta},\qquad
 m^2=v(1-\theta)=\frac{v^2}{v+\lambda/n}.
\]
The last expression increases with $v$ and $n$ and decreases with
$\lambda$. This proves both moment bounds, including the limit
$n\longrightarrow\infty$ in the upper bound. Conditional centering gives
$\beta=(1-\theta)(1-2p)+\mu_+(\mathbb R)-\mu_-(\mathbb R)$,
where the two nonnegative masses sum to $\theta$. Hence
\[
 1-2p-2(1-p)\theta\le\beta\le1-2p+2p\theta.
\]
Replacing each coefficient outward on $p\in[l,h]$ proves the stated
conservative bounds. The argument also covers zero noise and does not
assume $p\le1/2$.
\end{proof}

For a closed rectangle
$|H|\in[h_0,h_1]$, $\lambda\in[\lambda_0,\lambda_1]$, discard a moment
cell only if its $p,m,\beta$ range is disjoint from the necessary ranges,
or if its proved upper bound on $|H|$ is strictly below $h_0$.
The noise filter uses only $\lambda_1$. For every remaining cell, clip
$h_1$ by that cell's phase ceiling. Maximize each concave quadratic in
\eqref{pc:cell} at the two endpoints and, when included, its vertex.
Take the maximum over both target endpoints and every remaining cell,
and the cellwise minimum with $T$. Call the result $S_\epsilon$.
This bounds $\mathcal U$ throughout the closed rectangle. All equalities
at seams and all intersecting moment cells remain included. If no cell
remains, the rectangle contains no law allowed by the ancestor cover.
If $S_\epsilon<0$, nonnegativity of $\mathcal U$ excludes the rectangle.
