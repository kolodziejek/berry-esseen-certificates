\begin{abstract}
We study the conjectured sharp iid Berry--Esseen constant
$\CE=(3+\sqrt{10})/(6\sqrt{2\pi})$ by maximizing excess over this fixed
target. Attainment, contact and a conservation identity reduce a possible
counterexample to one actual marginal with a common moment-defect budget.
An explicitly stated Fourier certificate supplies the coarse entry.
For $n\ge700$, elementary moment inequalities and two contact rays then
acquire one probability and support strip. A single-count fourth-moment
argument handles small conditional noise; five counts and two concave
quartics handle the remaining noise. Both retain the actual marginal,
all threshold phases and atoms, and the complete incoming tails.
The resulting acquired-witness exclusion uses the true sharp predecessor.
The global implication is conditional on a genuine finite base through
$699$ and the complete entry predicates. Neither that base nor the full
conjecture is proved here. This manuscript integrates the one-strip
reductions and their analytical proofs in a single route.
\end{abstract}
