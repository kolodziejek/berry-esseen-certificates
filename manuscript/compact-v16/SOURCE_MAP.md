## Selected dependency chain

1. Fixed-target attainment chooses one actual standardized law and a positive
   closed-event orientation. Contact acquires compact support before higher
   moments are used. Reflection includes the atom inequality and does not copy
   a signed defect budget to the reflected law.
2. Zero-bias integration gives conservation with coefficient `(n+H²)a` and the
   actual support-gap defect `D=(3+sqrt(10))*rho-gamma-3J`. Contact residuals and
   endpoint conventions remain explicit.
3. The retained structural inequality, both quadratic entry passes and all
   four signed entry passes supply the coarse same-law bounds, including
   `nD <= 32.20665378` and raw `|H| <= 1.83605680`.
4. At `n >= 700`, direct moment geometry acquires the actual central gap,
   `3/8 < p < 93/200`, inward sum below `1/250` and noise `lambda < 7/20`.
   With the genuine sharp predecessor and the retained contact/heat bounds,
   two whole rays give conditional amplitude `3/5` on this one interval.
5. For `0 < lambda <= 1/100`, one count and the complete fourth-moment incoming
   tail prove strict negative excess. For `1/100 <= lambda <= 7/20`, five
   counts, four centered replacements and four rational endpoints do so.
   Both use the same actual marginal and raw threshold. At zero noise,
   Schulz's separate strict finite-binomial theorem applies directly.
6. A genuine base through 699 and the retained entry implications at a least
   failure give the conditional global conclusion. The fixed Bernoulli limit
   supplies the independent sharp lower bound.

## Remaining mathematical boundary

The local strip theorem is unconditional within its own explicit law domain.
Its acquisition from an extremal witness is conditional on the true sharp
predecessor and retained entry/contact/heat inputs. The manuscript therefore
does **not** assert an unconditional cutoff at 700.

- With a genuine BASE699 and the full retained entry at every least failure,
  the stated global sharp conclusion follows.
- With only BASE41, a first failure would have to be in 42–699. Those indices
  still need a genuine finite base or a separate complete argument.
- Independent C4, BASE41, BASE699 and full-conjecture acceptance are not
  supplied by this editorial integration.
