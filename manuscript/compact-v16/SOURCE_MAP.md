   signed passes give the same-law entry bounds `nD <= 32.20665378` and raw
   `|H| <= 1.83605680`. All six Fourier HIGH terms and the full LOW triple remain.
4. At `n >= 700`, direct geometry gives `3/8 < p < 93/200`, inward sum below
   `1/250` and `lambda < 7/20`. Two whole contact rays, using the true sharp
   predecessor, give conditional amplitude `3/5`.
5. The exact normalized Bernoulli-plus-noise marginal is the original law,
   with the same raw threshold. One count covers `0 < lambda <= 1/100` and
   five counts cover `1/100 <= lambda <= 7/20`. Both retain all atoms, phase
   values and complete tails. Zero noise uses the separate strict-binomial
   theorem, not a limiting argument.
6. A genuine BASE699 and the retained entry predicates at every least failure
   give the conditional global conclusion. The fixed Bernoulli example gives
   the independent sharp lower approximation. BASE41 alone leaves 42–699 open.

## Why the omitted lemmas and branches are unnecessary here

The unused sharp third-moment `rho(gamma)` factorization, its general sharp
family/equality cases and the old simultaneous-gap theorem have no consumer
in the selected route. The actual 13-file entry execution path uses the
retained signed Cauchy, physical moment circle, PSD fourth-moment corrections
and ordered costs. The acquisition proves the stronger common-gap budget it
needs directly. This dependency conclusion was checked against the selected
native call path, not merely by searching TeX references.

Direct acquisition replaces downstream probability panels and ray tables.
The two complete noise comparisons replace the old numerical noise tables,
Bernstein, harmonic/marked/curvature and compound-Poisson subtrees on this
`n >= 700` route. Wider Left/Right rows and alternative smaller-index branches
remain at their original source locations and scopes.

The **11,264 nominal entry boxes, both coarse passes, all four signed passes
and the internal 100-panel incidence contract remain required**. Omitting
post-entry panels does not remove internal entry coverage. Reflection chooses
orientation before acquisition and never transfers a signed budget between laws.
