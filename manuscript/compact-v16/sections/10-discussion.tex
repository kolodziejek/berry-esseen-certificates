\section{Discussion and reproducibility}\label{ms:discussion}
The fixed target aligns the coefficient in extremality, contact and the
predecessor estimate. Conservation prices departures from two points
against one physical budget. After coarse entry, direct inequalities
acquire one strip and two relative-noise estimates finish the comparison.
Their bounds stay relative to $\lambda$ down to zero; zero noise itself
uses the strict binomial theorem.

The unresolved issue is finite: a genuine base through $699$, or another
argument closing the gap from a smaller base, is still needed. This work
provides neither an independent four-summand proof nor a complete base
through $41$.

authors are responsible for the content and presentation.
