\subsection{Reading the argument}\label{ms:guide}\label{ms:notation}
The proof selects an extremizer, identifies the zero-defect law, bounds
the remainder in a conservation identity, and compares the resulting
near-Bernoulli law with a binomial sum. Reflection selects the positive
closed-event orientation before localization; its signed defect is
estimated in that orientation. The finite Fourier calculation is used
only to obtain the coarse bounds. The subsequent support and count
arguments are analytical, with explicit rational comparisons.

The following table distinguishes the three scales used in the proof.
Gap-specific parameters are introduced after the existence of a central
gap has been established.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}P{.27\linewidth}P{.66\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning and scale \\
\midrule
$H$, $H/\sqrt n$ & Threshold for the original sum; its normal-standardized value. \\
$B_0=\ind_{\{X>0\}}$, $p,q$ & Sign class and its probabilities for the original marginal. \\
$m$, $\ell=m/(pq)$ & $m=\E X_+=\E X_-$; distance between conditional means. \\
$W$, $\theta=\E W^2$ & $W=X-\E(X\mid B_0)$; conditional noise in original units. \\
$U=W/\ell$, $s=\E U^2$, $\lambda=ns$ & Noise in count units; marginal and total noise variances. \\
$t=np+H\sqrt{pq+s}=k+u$ & Count threshold, integer anchor, and phase. \\
$a_0,a_1$, $d=a_0+a_1$ & Inward support displacements in count units. \\
$J$, $D$ & Weighted support-gap sum and defect of the original law. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The sign decomposition is the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{revision:canonical-decomposition}
 X=\ell(B_0+U-p)=\frac{B_0+U-p}{\sqrt{pq+s}},\qquad
 pq+s=\ell^{-2},\qquad \E(U\mid B_0)=0.
\end{equation}
Indeed the conditional means are $-p\ell$ and $q\ell$, and the variance
decomposition gives $1=pq\ell^2+\theta$.
Thus the normalized count-coordinate variable is exactly $X$, and $H$
remains its original raw threshold. The small-noise proof chooses an
anchor with $u\in[-3/10,7/10]$; the interpolation proof chooses the floor
anchor, with $u\in[0,1)$. These are separate choices for separate estimates.
The signed moments are $\gamma=\E X^3$ and $\beta=\E[X|X|]$.

The main text retains the variational, geometric, localization, and
comparison arguments. The appendices give the full Fourier bound,
constant enclosures, the identity without contact, and reproducibility
instructions. The complete localization theorem is stated before its
technical derivation; all its stronger auxiliary conclusions are retained.
