\subsection{Reading the proof}\label{ms:guide}\label{ms:notation}
The argument follows one marginal through four stages. The distinction
between acquiring hypotheses and applying the resulting comparison is
useful when reading the longer calculations.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Section~\ref{v9:core:section} selects an attained fixed-target
maximum and derives contact and conservation. The support becomes compact
before fourth and higher moments enter.
\item Sections~\ref{ms:geometry-counts} and~\ref{v9:entry:section}
turn conservation into coarse bounds for its physical gap defect and raw
threshold. The entry includes explicit finite certificate conditions;
Appendix~\ref{v9:fourier:section} supplies their analytical Fourier bounds.
\item Section~\ref{short:acq:section} uses those coarse bounds to acquire
one support strip. Its moment geometry is independent of contact; the two
outer support rays use contact and the actual sharp predecessor.
\item Section~\ref{short:count:section} compares every law in that strip
with a binomial law. It needs only the strip hypotheses. Section~\ref{short:composition}
then states precisely how this local exclusion composes with a finite base.
\end{enumerate}

There are two coordinate systems. The physical random variable $X$ is
centered and has variance one. Its sign indicator is $B_0=\ind_{\{X>0\}}$,
with $p=\Pp(B_0=1)$ and $q=1-p$. Subtract the conditional means and divide
by their separation $\ell$ to obtain the count noise $U$. The exact relation is
\[
 X=\ell(B_0+U-p),\qquad \E(U\mid B_0)=0.
\]
The following quantities will recur; each is defined again where used.
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
 \theta & \E\{X-\E(X\mid B_0)\}^2\\
 s & \E U^2=\theta/\ell^2\\
 \lambda & ns\\
 a_0,a_1 & \text{inward support displacements in count coordinates}\\
 D & (3+\sqrt{10})\rho-\gamma-3J
\end{array}
\]
Here $\gamma=\E X^3$, $\beta=\E[X|X|]$, and $J$ is the actual weighted
support-gap sum. The letters $d=a_0+a_1$ and $D$ have different roles:
$d$ measures inward displacement, whereas $D$ is the physical moment defect.
The normalization satisfies $pq+s=\ell^{-2}$, so reconstruction leaves
both the actual law and the raw threshold $H$ unchanged.

Reflection chooses the positive closed-event orientation before entry.
All subsequent signed quantities are acquired in that orientation.
Alternative smaller-index routes and wider comparison domains are preserved
in the repository; they are not extra premises of this selected route.
