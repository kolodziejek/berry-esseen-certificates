\section{The sharp constant and the proof route}\label{ms:introduction}\label{ms:scope}
For centered, variance-one laws $P$ with $\rho(P)=\E|X|^3<\infty$, let
$S_n$ be a sum of independent copies and set
\[
 \Delta_n(P)=\sup_{H\in\R}|\Pp_P(S_n\le H)-\Phi(H/\sqrt n)|,
 \qquad C_n=\sup_P\frac{\sqrt n\Delta_n(P)}{\rho(P)}.
\]
The iid sharp-constant conjecture is
\begin{equation}\label{ms:conjecture}
 \sup_{n\ge1}C_n=\CE,
 \qquad \CE=\frac{3+\sqrt{10}}{6\sqrt{2\pi}}.
\end{equation}
Here $\Phi,\phi$ denote the standard normal distribution function and
density; $H$ is always a raw sum threshold. Finite decimals mean exact
rationals. Affine standardization gives the usual arbitrary-mean,
positive-variance form.

The classical Berry--Esseen theorem gives the order $n^{-1/2}$
\cite{Berry1941,Esseen1942}. Esseen's fixed Bernoulli example gives the
sharp lower approximation \cite{Esseen1956}. Namely, for
$p_*=(4-\sqrt{10})/2$, $q_*=1-p_*$ and
$X=(B-p_*)/\sqrt{p_*q_*}$,
\begin{equation}\label{ms:bernoulli-limit}
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt n\Delta_n(P)}{\rho(P)}
 =\frac{3+q_*-p_*}{6\sqrt{2\pi}(p_*^2+q_*^2)}=\CE.
\end{equation}
The last equality uses $p_*^2+q_*^2=10-3\sqrt{10}$.
Thus one fixed two-point law already makes every constant smaller than
$\CE$ fail for sufficiently large $n$. The remaining task is the upper
bound, uniformly over both the marginal and the index. Strict inequalities
at each finite index are compatible with this limiting lower bound.
The universal upper conjecture is associated with Zolotarev
\cite{Zolotarev1966}. We use Shevtsova's structural moment bound
\cite{Shevtsova2013} and Schulz's strict finite-binomial theorem
\cite{Schulz2016}, both stated where applied.

Fix $c=\CE$ and maximize the excess
\begin{equation}\label{v9:intro:excess}
 a(P,H)=\sqrt n\{\Pp_P(S_n\le H)-\Phi(H/\sqrt n)\}-c\rho(P).
\end{equation}
The choice of objective matters. Its target $c$ is fixed before the law
is varied, so the same coefficient appears in the variational inequality,
the conservation identity and the predecessor estimate. The third-moment
penalty also controls escape of mass in a maximizing sequence. These two
features let us study an attained law and then extract information from
its support.

The fixed moment penalty gives attainment and compact-support contact.
Integrating contact against the zero-bias density gives one budget for
excess, moment defect and leakage. Complete coarse Fourier entry supplies
$nD\le32.20665378$. At $n\ge700$, direct geometry and two contact rays
then acquire one probability/support strip. Two comparisons, split at
noise $\lambda=1/100$, exclude its nonnegative excess.

All steps retain the same actual marginal. Reflection chooses its
positive closed-event orientation before signed acquisition. The local
comparison itself requires neither contact nor a predecessor; these are
used only to acquire its hypotheses. The global implication in
Section~\ref{short:composition} still needs a genuine base through
$699$: a base only through $41$ leaves $42,\ldots,699$ unresolved.
