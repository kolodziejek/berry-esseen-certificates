## Fixed algebraic family

The sparse coefficient ring is `Q[x,a,b,r,t,u,v,g]`. Variable meanings are local to the named target: in central-gap targets `a=A,b=B,r=rho`; in conditional targets `x=|X|,a=|E[X|sign]|,t=rho±gamma_V`; in Gram-projection targets `b=beta,r=rho,g=gamma,u=mu4`; in a support-localizing matrix `r` is that sign's radius. The Hessian square uses arbitrary displacement coordinatesu,v. No sign assumption on the conditional parameter t is made.

The complete fixed target list is specified by `fixed_targets.targets()`. It consists of P1,P2,G, their eight relevant derivatives/substitutions, the signed quadraticF and all first/second derivatives, its stationary value and Hessian quadratic, two outer square identities, the two equal Gram-projection determinant expressions, and conditional Taylor quotients of orders2–5. Candidate coefficients cannot choose a different target. Closed-domain validity uses the explicit analytic domains, not a generic claim that every variable is nonnegative.

The seven matrices are H0–H3 with weightx^j and L0–L2 with weightx^j(r−x). They are PSD on the complete interval0<=x<=r for each actual sign measure. The supplied entries are verified as weight times1,x,x². Taking expectations then gives the signwise moment/support contractions. A zero Schur divisor forces its cross entry to zero; the map never divides by it. A merely inadequate interval bound can make a map inapplicable, and must not exclude the law automatically.

The25 Bernstein rows cover the entire closed square, including zero-width physical rectangles after affine substitution. All25 inverse identities are checked. Signed multiplication bym retains both endpoint products. The maximum is convex inrho because each central coefficient is affine inrho; the checker does not infer convexity of an arbitrary interval-selected minimum.
