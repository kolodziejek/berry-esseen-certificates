# Result of the next shortening experiment

The complete revised PDF has **98 pages**, down from 102. The approximately
60-page goal is still unmet. The experiment supplies evidence against one
proposed route to a large saving; it does not show that a shorter proof is
impossible.

coefficient replacement also grew from 7,592 to 8,474 source characters for

needed all explanations about signs, zero denominators, conditional variance,
the same marginal, actual gap integrals and the shared budget. Introducing
that acceptance interface did not help the reader or the page count.

The preceding v17 had 194 total pages, and the previously measured public
original had 159. Those counts include their analytic appendices. This
revision preserves the earlier substantial consolidation and makes a further
modest improvement; it does not conceal a longer indispensable supplement.

## Implemented changes and mathematical scope

**Sections 5.8 and 7.3: one Pearson envelope and one paired integral.** The
signed fifth-moment envelope follows from the nonnegative measure
`(X²−gamma X−1)² dP`. One proof now supplies both the triangle and positive
rectangle formulas. The Gaussian norm integral is computed once; a finite
integration-by-parts recurrence supplies the Mills bounds. All original
coefficient extrema, remainder constants and index factors remain. The
`43` damping specialization is confined to fourth-moment ceiling at most
two; the general ceiling uses `45−M`.

**Section 7.2: a shorter index-transfer proof.** The invariant remains the
pair `R_n<=r` and `nD<=Nd`, for one attaining law with ratio at least the
sharp constant. The proof groups decreasing Fourier factors, score/curvature
updates, weighted physical resources and phase/variance exports. It retains
the raw sign license `r<.4N`, all positive denominator gates, both phase signs,
and actual sample sizes in support and binomial calculations. A positive
`K*rho` charge is a separate excess lower bound, never added to another bound
on the same excess. The already established conditional gamma inequality
is cited instead of proved twice.

**Sections 6.7–6.8: a complete application-region table.** The table shows
the closed moment joins between count comparisons, supported/unrestricted
Fourier bounds and the affine tail. Native score/HIGH arrays, literal pass
schedules and forest counts remain certificate data. Their analytic meaning
stays in the paper: complete coverage, same-law export, directed simultaneous
containment, all original count roots and actual indices. Positive39 remains
a prerequisite to sharp39. The explicit phase directions, closed seams,
finite-binomial reserve and five weighted panel exclusions remain proved.

The all-index theorem and strictness range `n>=47` are unchanged. There is
no new claim of strictness at 42–46 or eventual exact two-point optimality.

**Section 7.6: explain the binomial increment at every boundary.** The
curvature proof now uses the `Bin(n+1,p)` mass
`pi_j^+=q*pi_j+p*pi_(j-1)<=max_i pi_i` and the exact identity
`pi_j-pi_(j-1)=((n+1)*p-j)*pi_j^+/((n+1)*p*q)`.
This proves the same coefficient bound for every integer, including zero
extension, without a boundary denominator. It is the one direct prose
improvement adopted from the structural pilot; no experimental bound is used.

## New bounded audit

The optional coefficient family has 28 fixed polynomial identities, seven
weighted moment-matrix families and 25 inverse tensor-Bernstein identities.
Its checker fixes the mathematical targets independently of the proposed
objects. Tests reject 75 corrupted objects and five invalid applicability
domains, and include seven valid boundary cases. A domain rejection says
the proposed rule is inapplicable; it does not exclude an actual law.

All 83 selected signed-resource rows give exactly the native outputs in
166 central-bound calls and 83 signed conditional-error calls. A separate
primitive moment derivation also matches four outputs in every one of
87,963 reached INITIAL47 algebra-prefix rows across 14 input states. The
portable prefix replay reproduces the saved compressed rows byte for byte.
No Fourier integral, feedback state or original numerical export is accepted
by these new audits. Some generic map enclosures can be weaker than a
specialized old bound; equality is claimed only for the named comparisons.

An independent source/analytic review also checks exact rational laws and
negative controls. Such examples support the review; the continuous-domain
licenses are the mathematical arguments. None of this optional machinery is
a new ancestor of the Berry–Esseen theorem.
