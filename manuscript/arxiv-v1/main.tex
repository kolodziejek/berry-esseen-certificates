\title{Toward the sharp iid Berry--Esseen inequality:\\
analytic reductions and finite certificates}
\author{Bartosz Ko{\l}odziejek \and Kacper Rodziewicz}
\date{First manuscript draft, 27 October 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
The conjectured optimal constant in the iid Berry--Esseen inequality is
$\CE=(3+\sqrt{10})/(6\sqrt{2\pi})$. A proof must control every summand law
and every sample size, including discrete laws close to the extremal
two-point distribution. We develop an analytic framework for reducing this
problem to explicitly stated finite certificate conditions. At a least
index of failure beyond a sharp base through $41$, the predecessor loss
vanishes. A uniform Gaussian smoothing estimate then bounds the current score. This permits a short support
argument without Bernstein positivity certificates. We also give an exact
three-candidate formula for a curvature supremum in the conditional-count
comparison, and show how retaining the score excess strengthens the joint
defect bound and removes some numerical comparisons. The analytic arguments
are separated from the certificate conditions and their implementation.
The global implication is conditional: this draft does not assert completion
of the finite base, the remaining local certificates, or their full
composition. The new reductions and their proofs are presented independently
of such a completion.
\end{abstract}
