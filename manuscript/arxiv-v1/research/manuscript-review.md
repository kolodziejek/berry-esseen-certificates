## Review outcome

The independent reviews found no unresolved blocking error in the **scoped new
conditional implications**. They support circulation as a first research draft,
not a claim of a proved sharp constant. The constructor/reviewer overlap is
explicit in each report; agreement among agents is not mathematical evidence.

- `core-review.md`: independently rederived attainment/contact, smoothing,
  physical defect/joint plane, count/crossing identities and score charge.
  Eight rational laws,288 count/collision cases,96 coupled cases and3,024
  discrete-curvature cases passed; a strict/closed-event mutation was detected.
- `independent-review.md`: new support, curvature, moment domains,
  normalization and conditional global composition; limits on reviewing the
  reviewer's own earlier bridge/resource work are disclosed.
- `finite-support-integration-review.md`: independently derived direct
  support, heat/far/series arguments. All six n5 rational ray gates pass;
  the six pairs of whole-H endpoint inequalities remain unproved.
- `finite-integration-review.md`: N1 expectation/tails, G3 condition and G4
  dependency, n5 structural cutoff, weighted floor invariant and the three
  signed local42 source notes;14 exact scalar checks passed.

The n5 structural tail cutoff has **equality** at819/2000, not a strict reserve
there. The subsequent target comparison is strict. The six n5 support rows
are instantiated in the finite appendix with explicit S,E,c and beta caps;
this proves their rational ray gates but does not establish their whole-H
endpoint conditions. The coarse far-Gaussian bound is too weak at those
endpoints and is not silently substituted for them.

1. BASE41 is not established by this manuscript. The current pinned4042
   provider remains unavailable in the inspected closure, and older joins
   consume tail/coarse-ceiling inputs. An independent base must be shown.
2. The inherited full Fourier/confinement/finite-index analytic chain has not
   received complete independent verification. Source transcription, compiling,
   and agreeing reviewers do not discharge it.
3. Complete actual-law acquisition and every selected local predicate at the
   new floor remain obligations. Literal finite-index bounds and floor47
   statements are preserved; no certificate gains a new scope from its name.
4. The changed curvature/resource outputs and150 LOW rows need a new complete
   acceptance contract. The separate152-row byte-preserving prototype does not
   accept them. The169-task candidate count is for one component, not the whole
   proof and not a measured runtime saving.
5. No native generation campaign, full historical certificate replay,
   third-party Lean build or end-to-end acceptance was run for this manuscript.
6. External theorem inputs retain their bibliographic and retrieval limits in
   the classical/modern source reports. In particular, Schulz's binomial theorem
   is an external input; there is no assertion of a fresh complete thesis audit.
