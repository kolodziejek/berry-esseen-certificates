Schulz's finite-binomial theorem. The new manuscript does not claim to prove
the latter from a hash. The original full thesis should be available during
the final author review; the inherited locator Remark4.1 was not independently

in `modern-a.md` to Shevtsova2014. Its preprint calls the relevant statement
Theorem2; the unverified journal locator “Theorem6” is not retained. The
manuscript supplies the elementary factorization proof.

## Decisions that prevent duplicate or misleading citations

- English translations and Russian originals are not presented as different
  improvements. The cited edition determines the year and page numbers.
- The old arXiv title of He–Cheng is not a second paper; version3 is the
  primary manuscript citation and the earlier version is mentioned only to
  explain the formalization snapshot.
- Repository claims remain attributed claims where full independent replay
  was not performed. An arXiv DOI is not evidence of journal publication.
- Websites locating manuscripts are not counted as independent mathematical
  sources. Use the pinned manuscript and, where relevant, a separate software
  citation for its distinct role.
- No citation to the present authors' earlier Hexagon text is used to
  discharge an analytic or numerical obligation in the present draft.
