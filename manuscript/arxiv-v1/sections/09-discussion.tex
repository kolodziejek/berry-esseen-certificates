\section{Discussion}\label{ms:discussion}

Fixing the target changes the order of the proof. The predecessor bound
and the coefficient in the variational problem now agree from the start,
and the conservation identity retains the error excess throughout the
calculation. Signed moment feedback makes a shorter coarse acquisition
possible. At small noise, a continuous contact argument replaces a further
probability grid. These are proved reductions at their stated scopes.

The remaining difficulty is the convolution comparison. A small moment
defect alone does not prevent conditional lattice resonance, as the
explicit family in Section~\ref{v9:harmonic:section} shows. Contact-based
confinement therefore has a role that cannot be replaced by a favorable
moment box. Even after confinement, the error estimates must stay relative
to $\lambda$ as $\lambda$ tends to zero. An absolute remainder of order
$n^{-3/2}$ is not sufficient for that purpose.

The conditional global argument has no large terminal index: a least
failure supplies its own sharp predecessors. To complete it, one still
needs both an independent finite base and a local exclusion at every
later index. The statements in this paper keep those two obligations
separate from the intermediate results already proved.

\subsection*{Use of artificial intelligence}

AI systems were used extensively in the research and in writing this
paper. ChatGPT and Codex assisted with mathematical exploration, exact
checking programs, source and bibliography comparisons, and drafting and
revising the text. Other AI systems assisted with mathematical and
editorial review. Some proposed arguments failed under checking; they
were not used as premises. The authors are responsible for the content
and presentation. Agreement between AI systems is not treated as
mathematical verification.

The work builds in part on an earlier AI-assisted draft on
Hexagon~\cite{rodziewiczkolodziejek2026momentdefects}. The present version
gives the fixed-target argument and the analytical details of its stated
reductions, with the remaining obligations listed in
Section~\ref{ms:certificates}.
