\section{Remaining work and provenance}\label{ms:discussion}

The main obstacle is now the connection between a short analytic
reduction and a complete collection of finite statements at their actual
scopes. The scalar argument does not require an astronomical terminal
index. Its force comes from the order of the integers: at a first
contrary index the predecessor estimates are already sharp. To use this
observation, however, the finite base must be independent of the tail
that it is meant to initiate, and the local comparison must be uniform
at the new floor. Establishing those two facts is more consequential
than reducing a numerical cutoff without changing the proof structure.

The next mathematical review should follow the dependencies in that
order. It should close the finite-base source graph, check the retained
Fourier and confinement arguments with their signs and constants, and
then verify that the numerical cover expresses those exact hypotheses.
The comparison near zero defect deserves particular attention: an
absolute error of order $n^{-3/2}$ is not automatically small relative
to an arbitrarily small defect. The explicit conditional-count formulas
are intended to retain the relative information that this regime needs.

The new reductions also suggest a practical principle for computational
proofs. A numerical search should begin after the analytic dependencies
have been simplified. Here an elementary Gaussian estimate removes a
support positivity calculation, a piecewise derivative calculation removes
a curvature mesh, and a conserved score term makes some parameter cells
empty. These conclusions are useful even before the full sharp theorem
is available, because their proofs describe precisely which work is
unnecessary and which assumptions still have to be supplied.

\subsection*{Use of artificial intelligence}

AI systems were used extensively in the research and in preparing this
manuscript. ChatGPT and Codex assisted with mathematical exploration,
code for exact checks, source comparisons, bibliography work, and drafting
and revising the exposition. This revision also considered model-generated
editorial suggestions supplied by the authors. Proposed arguments and
citations were checked before adoption; suggestions that changed a claim's
scope were not treated as editorial corrections.

The earlier working draft~\cite{rodziewiczkolodziejek2026momentdefects}
records the project's prior AI-assisted development. The present paper
separates the new analytic reductions from the remaining proof and
certificate obligations. The authors are responsible for the final
mathematical content; this draft remains subject to their review.
