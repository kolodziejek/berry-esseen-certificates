# Next substantive simplification

The next useful target is the earlier generic count argument. Replacing it
could remove repeated incoming/outgoing estimates, but no page saving is
established until every consumer of those estimates has a complete substitute.
The first campaign should therefore address one difficult complete original
row, not a collection of favorable points.

## What has been established

On the selected five-summand BN row with probability in [0.38,0.39], the
separately acquired inward bound gives spacing c=0.056934. At p=77/200,
k=2 and phase u=0, take U0=+/-1/10 equally and U1=0. This is one genuine
three-point iid law after normalization and satisfies the checked numerical
exports of that row. Its staircase expectation is exactly 31/246. Even
using its true moments, the resulting score proxy exceeds 0.456, while the
law's actual score is between 0.017 and 0.018. Consequently no polynomial
majorant of that same coarse staircase can close this point. This is an
obstruction to the proposed bound, not a Berry–Esseen counterexample or a
claim that the example is an attaining contact law.

A different bound uses one quartic P_j for each of the six actual counts:

    P_j(z) >= 1{z<=0},
    P(sum Y_i<=k+u) <= sum_j pi_j E P_j(W_j+j-k-u).

All expectations still use one common pair of conditional laws. With
x=q E U0² and y=p E U1², the acquired signed second moment satisfies

    beta = [pq(1-2p)-x+y]/[pq+x+y].

Its lower and upper bounds give two linear halfplanes in x,y. Together
with nonnegativity and the noise cap they form a compact polygon. Three
skew/inward endpoints for each class give nine common moment choices.
The finite-sample identity and Cauchy's inequality give the positive
quadratic fourth-moment term 3(1-1/n)t_j². The resulting mixture is convex
in x,y, so its maximum is controlled by polygon vertices.

This proves an exact pilot at n=5, p=77/200, k=2, u=0 throughout the original
noise interval [0,7687/25000] and signed beta interval
[-15293/500000,307221/1000000]. Six quartics pass all twelve global ray
checks and thirty-six common-moment tests, with a CDF margin greater than
0.00919. Separate exact acceptance and corruption controls pass. **The
probability and phase are fixed; this is not a complete original row.**

## Proposed next complete campaign — not yet proved

Use the original n=5, BN, probability-panel-38 contract, whose moment band
is [11/10,23/20]. Freeze its source identity, actual target, signed moment
bounds, support endpoints, original joint defect budget and separate
physical inward/noise caps before discovery. Retain the original orientation;
do not replace a probability above 1/2 by its reflection without handling
the corresponding CDF convention.

The required cover is p in [19/50,39/100], every licensed integer nearest
threshold k, both closed phase halves of [-1/2,1/2], and the full acquired
noise domain. Derive the complete range of k from the original H, p and
scale bounds, including the outer threshold branches. The six mixture
counts j=0,...,5 do not by themselves restrict k to that range. Any phase,
moment or resource exclusion must be proved from the same original row.
Count ties and atoms on event planes remain included.
The exact target must be bound before search; a numerical margin obtained
by weakening it is not a replacement certificate.

For a closed p cell, construct one rational outer polygon independent of
p by replacing the right sides of the beta halfplanes by rigorous upper
bounds on that whole cell. These right sides are the explicit cubic
polynomials

    pq[(1-2p)-beta_lower],
    pq[beta_upper-(1-2p)].

Their Bernstein coefficients give a direct rational enclosure. Retain
every polygon vertex. Use common outward endpoint bounds for the inward
and skew choices so the moment corners also have fixed, licensed domains.
At each vertex and common skew pair, compute the six exact binomial masses
and translated quartic expectations. Multiplication by the positive
denominator p²q² produces polynomial inequalities of degree at most nine
in p and four in u. A tensor Bernstein cover can verify these inequalities
on each closed p/u cell, alongside the twelve global CDF-majorant ray tests.
Use signed Gaussian enclosures on the entire cell rather than copying the
fixed-point monotonicity argument when its numerator changes sign.

The first deliverable should be **one complete source-bound row**: all
licensed threshold roots, the whole closed probability/phase/noise domain,
six count majorants at every retained cell, complete acceptance and
missing/corrupted-cover controls. Only
then should the method be tested on the other retained n=5 rows and later
finite indices. Failure on a cell may require the original common rho,
mean or physical-defect restrictions; it is not evidence against the main
inequality. A full replacement and its net page saving remain unproved.

standard-library replay, with the precise fixed-p/phase scope. It is outside
