## Readability and measured scope

The old zero-noise rescaling/tail discussion occupies about one page, and the old six-branch uniform TINY parameter description about a quarter page. The new coupling/moment proof plus four-rectangle specification is of comparable length. Because the finite small-noise branches still require the old proof, inserting the new result immediately is likely a **page increase**, not a decrease. No full replacement build or net saving is claimed by this audit.

The genuinely new mathematical gain is a common finite polynomial expectation rule that has already replaced the incoming-tail machinery on all uniform TINY rows. The next deletion-enabling gate is to accept the finite small-noise domains with this same rule, keeping their actual constants and positive allowances. Eliminating the larger tail/whole-count proof additionally requires full replacement of its remaining MID/LOW cells; merely extending uniform coverage to `.08` is insufficient.
