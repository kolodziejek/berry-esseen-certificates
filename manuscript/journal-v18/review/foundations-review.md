## Equality at the sharp constant

The proposed removal of the compact fixed-target route depends on an actual boundary argument, not continuity in C_n. I checked the following points independently of the extension's prose conclusion.

1. **Attainment at equality.** The new core proves ratio attainment for every fixed n>=2 and C_n>0, so C_n=C_E supplies an attained law. The closed-event reflection and moving-threshold argument include atoms. At that law the contact coefficient is C_n and a_c=0; compact support does not require positive excess.
2. **Support starts at zero barrier.** In the old support-polynomial proof, K>0 is an optional stronger finite-band hypothesis. The next paragraph explicitly supplies the unrestricted choice K=0,c=.4095. Thus the positive-barrier branch is not silently used for equality.
3. **The actual INITIAL47 input contains zero.** `accept_initial47.py` fixes `barrier=Fraction(0)` and declares C_E<=C_n<=.4247. In `local_signed_core.py`, `init` accepts0<=barrier; `stage` uses K only in c0=.4095+K/nmax and the subtraction K*rho_lower. With K=0 there is no positive compulsory excess. The stored nmax98 constrains a preparation field, not the actual-index theorem; the separate analytic floor transfer and infinite support row remain necessary.
4. **No concealed positive-excess division.** The native signed stage drops a cell when `raw/n - PHI*(1-5*raw/(2n))*dlower/6 < 0`, using only nonnegative excess. Its score division is by rho_lower>=1. Other divisions use explicitly positive conditional means, second-moment denominators or quadratic curvature. The raw and defect invariants in INITIAL47 are positive **upper bounds**, not assumptions that the law's actual excess or defect is positive.
5. **The raw sign license survives.** At a ratio maximizer,

   `phi(H/sqrt(n))*(gamma+3J)/6 = C_n*rho + sqrt(n)*L - R/n`.

   Consequently R<.4n and C_n*rho>=C_E>.4 imply positivity. The positive conservation identity has e=C_n-C_E only as the nonnegative term n e rho; retaining equality is valid before a central gap is acquired. No division by e,D or L is used.
6. **The local comparison only needs a positive score floor.** In the original local comparison, C>=c_*>0 with selected c_*=.4. Its exact count identity compares the perturbed law with its own binomial law. The nearest-count and whole-count tests require their displayed sign gates before replacing probability masses or n by bounds. Neither requires C>C_E. The reviewed tiny47 and low47 normalizers use .4 for the correction; no excess lower bound appears in those normalizations.
7. **Arbitrarily small positive noise is not replaced by zero.** The TINY route retains its noise-relative quotient and positive-noise variance normalization. Its domain is0<lambda<=.05, including arbitrarily small lambda, not lambda>=epsilon. Thus the strict loss `rho_B(C_n-C_B)<-.001 lambda` remains strict at every positive lambda; an additive error independent of lambda would not suffice and is not introduced by the equality argument.
8. **Zero noise has a strict external bound.** When lambda=0, the exact sign normalization gives U=0 almost surely and X=(B-p)/sqrt(pq),0<p<1. I checked the primary theorem statement: [Schulz's thesis, Theorem1, printed page1/PDF page11](https://d-nb.info/1197702695/34) gives a strict finite-binomial inequality for every positive integer n and p in(0,1), with the factor C_E(p^2+q^2)/sqrt(npq). Hence C_B<C_E at every threshold. The strict sign is in the original statement; it is not inferred from asymptotic sharpness.

These checks establish the new boundary step, conditional on the unchanged all-phase, both-sign, complete-noise local cover and the complete analytic acquisition/confinement proof. Positive noise contradicts C_n>=C_E by comparison with C_B; zero noise contradicts it by the cited strict theorem. After the weak all-index bound, an assumed equality at n>=47 meets the predecessor, deleted-score, own-score and scaled-excess premises of the uniform proposition. Attainment then rules out equality of the supremum. This inference would fail without attainment, even if every individual law had a strict inequality.
