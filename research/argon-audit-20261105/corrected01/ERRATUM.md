# Wording clarification

In SOUNDNESS.md, the sentence beginning “Positivity of either alternate sufficient bound” should read “Positivity of either alternate polynomial.” The sign-reversed-price polynomial is a diagnostic expression, not a licensed sufficient certificate, even when its coefficients happen to be positive. The missing-price expression is licensed because zero prices are permitted.

The source, objects, arithmetic results and earlier proof-note bytes are unchanged. AUDIT.md already states this distinction explicitly.
