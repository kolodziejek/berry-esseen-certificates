## 2. Mathematical & Audit Mechanisms in `b3-band-20261106`

1. **Four-Endpoint-Pair Moment Reduction (`moment_pairs = 4`, `gaussian_taylor_both_halves_4pair_v1`):**
   After replacing fourth moments $\kappa_0, \kappa_1$ by their affine upper envelopes $K_0(\gamma_0), K_1(\gamma_1)$, the upper-bounded fourth-order Taylor score polynomial is affine separately in $(\gamma_0, \gamma_1) \in [g_{0,\text{lo}}, g_{0,\text{hi}}] \times [g_{1,\text{lo}}, g_{1,\text{hi}}]$. Therefore, 2 endpoints per conditional marginal (**4 corner pairs** per polygon vertex instead of 9) suffice to bound the entire moment rectangle.
2. **Shifted Support-Square Affine Fourth-Moment Envelopes:**
   From $(r_0 - U_0)(U_0 + A_0)(U_0 + d_{0,\text{in}})^2 \ge 0$ on $[-A_0, r_0]$ with $r_0 d_{0,\text{in}} \le s_{0,\min} \le s_0$ and $(A_1 - U_1)(U_1 + r_1)(U_1 - d_{1,\text{in}})^2 \ge 0$ on $[-r_1, A_1]$ with $r_1 d_{1,\text{in}} \le s_{1,\min} \le s_1$, taking expectations and dividing by $s_0, s_1 > 0$ yields the globally valid affine fourth-moment upper envelopes:
   $$K_0(\gamma_0) = (r_0 - A_0 - 2d_{0,\text{in}})\gamma_0 + A_0 r_0 - (A_0 - 2r_0)d_{0,\text{in}} - d_{0,\text{in}}^2,$$
   $$K_1(\gamma_1) = (A_1 - r_1 + 2d_{1,\text{in}})\gamma_1 + A_1 r_1 - (A_1 - 2r_1)d_{1,\text{in}} - d_{1,\text{in}}^2.$$
3. **Exact $\beta$-Forced Noise and Variance-Allocation Floors (`beta_floors` / `map_slab`):**
   On `T0` and `T2` panels `41..52` ($\beta_{\text{lo}} = 1/5$, $p > 2/5$), the polynomial $v(p)(1 - \beta_{\text{lo}} - 2p)$ has strictly negative upper Bernstein bound $M_{\beta,\text{lo}} < 0$ on $[p_l, p_u]$. Every $(x, y) \ge 0$ satisfying $(1 + \beta_{\text{lo}}) x - (1 - \beta_{\text{lo}}) y \le M_{\beta,\text{lo}} < 0$ therefore obeys
   $$5(x + y) \ge 5y \ge \text{lo}_b := \min\Big(\Lambda,\ \frac{-5 M_{\beta,\text{lo}}}{1 - \beta_{\text{lo}}}\Big) > 0, \qquad \frac{y}{x + y} > \alpha_b := \frac{1 + \beta_{\text{lo}}}{2} = \frac{3}{5}.$$
   When `noise` or `alpha` is subdivided, mapping `[lo, hi]` affinely from `[0, LAMBDA]` onto `[lo_b, LAMBDA]` and `[al, ah]` affinely from `[0, 1]` onto `[al_b, 1]` preserves the exact partition of the feasible polygon while ensuring non-empty child cells.
4. **Full-Corner Independent Polynomial and Cell-Geometry Audit (`review/check_b3_band_v3.py`):**
   Reconstructs every cell’s variance-allocation polygon and 4-pair shifted moment corners independently (`1,633` geometry checks) and verifies all 4 inverse Bernstein identities (`target`, `imp`, `ep`, `direct`) across **all 28,088 vertex-corner combinations** (`1,404,400` score coefficients and `112,352` inverse identities).
