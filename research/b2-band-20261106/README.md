## 2. Mathematical & Audit Upgrades Incorporated in `b2-band-20261106`

1. **Four-Endpoint-Pair Moment Reduction (`moment_pairs = 4`, `gaussian_taylor_both_halves_4pair_v1`):**
   After replacing fourth moments $\kappa_0, \kappa_1$ by their affine upper envelopes $K_0(\gamma_0), K_1(\gamma_1)$, the upper-bounded fourth-order Taylor score polynomial is affine separately in $(\gamma_0, \gamma_1) \in [g_{0,\text{lo}}, g_{0,\text{hi}}] \times [g_{1,\text{lo}}, g_{1,\text{hi}}]$. Therefore, 2 endpoints per conditional marginal (**4 corner pairs** per polygon vertex instead of 9) suffice to bound the entire moment rectangle.
2. **Shifted Support-Square Affine Fourth-Moment Envelopes:**
   From $(r_0 - U_0)(U_0 + A_0)(U_0 + d_{0,\text{in}})^2 \ge 0$ on $[-A_0, r_0]$ with $r_0 d_{0,\text{in}} \le s_{0,\min} \le s_0$ and $(A_1 - U_1)(U_1 + r_1)(U_1 - d_{1,\text{in}})^2 \ge 0$ on $[-r_1, A_1]$ with $r_1 d_{1,\text{in}} \le s_{1,\min} \le s_1$, taking expectations and dividing by $s_0, s_1 > 0$ yields the globally valid affine fourth-moment upper envelopes:
   $$K_0(\gamma_0) = (r_0 - A_0 - 2d_{0,\text{in}})\gamma_0 + A_0 r_0 - (A_0 - 2r_0)d_{0,\text{in}} - d_{0,\text{in}}^2,$$
   $$K_1(\gamma_1) = (A_1 - r_1 + 2d_{1,\text{in}})\gamma_1 + A_1 r_1 - (A_1 - 2r_1)d_{1,\text{in}} - d_{1,\text{in}}^2.$$
3. **Full-Corner Independent Polynomial and Cell-Geometry Audit (`review/check_b2_band_v3.py`):**
   Reconstructs every cell’s variance-allocation polygon and 4-pair shifted moment corners independently (`1,201` geometry checks) and verifies all 4 inverse Bernstein identities (`target`, `imp`, `ep`, `direct`) across **all 20,964 vertex-corner combinations** (`1,048,200` score coefficients and `83,856` inverse identities).
