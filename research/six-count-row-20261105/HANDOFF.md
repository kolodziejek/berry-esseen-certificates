p<1/2. A general reader needs v_min=min(l(1-l),h(1-h)) and v_max equal to
the largest endpoint value or 1/4 if the cell contains 1/2. Use those values
consistently in the Gaussian guard, denominator bounds, mean-derived noise
cap and absolute-cube denominator. Keep numerator signs explicit. Do not
simply delete the old p-side gate or reflect asymmetric acquired rows without
proving all sign/open-event transformations.

The moment bound uses n+1 different quartics, one common conditional pair,
and the coefficient 3(1-1/n). The polynomial degree is (n+4,4); a later-n
reader cannot retain V4's degree 9. The cubic price has a fixed rational
weight theta in [0,1] on each leaf. Its actual numerator is nonnegative;
relaxed corner evaluations need not represent a law. The rounding and
convexity argument must be performed on actual laws before enlargement.

Empty/degenerate polygons currently reject. They are not certified exclusions.
A general reader must either retain such domains or prove emptiness/handle
segments and points. The mean field is E X_+, not E|X|. The independent noise
cap is not the original affine resource intercept Lambda. These distinctions,
zero variances, moving thresholds, closed atoms and every original root remain
mandatory. Discovery infeasibility or a negative floating margin is not a proof.
