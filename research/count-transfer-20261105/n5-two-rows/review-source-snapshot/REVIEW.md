ceiling, sign-endpoint inward caps, sign-safe Gaussian bounds and the sharper
single-probability-endpoint rule. These work on the right half and at 1/2.
The root note's derivative, endpoint minimum and stronger concavity corollary
agree with the independent derivation. A failed threshold gate must retain
the variance-extrema fallback; the note states that restriction correctly.

The current model uses the older sufficient concavity test
`a_min²<=15*v_min` and the checked mean monotonicity gate
`2*v_min>m_lo²`. Both are valid conservative restrictions for the three
selected rows. They need not be weakened for correctness. The underlying
common-gamma, finite-Cauchy and polygon arguments are unchanged and retain
the same two conditional marginals in all six counts and the rho price.

The exact reader fixes the original contract identity externally, rebuilds
every moment row, requires six ordered quartics, and accepts both closed
children at every split. Malformed/failed leaves cannot accept. Its cell API
allows a phase superset of the clipped raw-threshold root; that is safe for a
conditional cell proof. Any complete-row collector must separately demand the
exact full root cover and original noise interval. Rejecting a degenerate
polygon is not a certificate of infeasibility.

The discovery driver only proposes coefficients and records unresolved
branches. Its failed LP or stopping condition is not a proof exclusion; the
