## Findings and repairs

1. **Source-binding omission, repaired.** The first acceptor pinned the new signed core and support helper but imported `signed-two-pass-check.py` without checking its hash. Thus its claim to a fixed ancestor was incomplete. The repaired `modules()` checks the two direct pins plus `PRELOAD_PINS` for nine ancestor/data files **before any constructor import**. These include the two-pass checker, moment and signed modules, first-cover checker/input, and all four pinned kernel files. I independently compared all11 pinned hashes with local bytes. The complete fixed contract is determined by the acceptor as well as the proof JSON; a proof hash alone is not that contract.
2. **Excluded-row schema ambiguity, repaired.** Python dictionary equality accepted `prior_excluded=1` in place of true and a floating index in place of an integer. On the first acceptor, both mutations at fourth-pass row16 passed `shape()`. They did not alter the numerical theorem, but violated the promised strict schema. The repair checks exact fields, `type(index) is int`, and `prior_excluded is True`. Fresh tests reject both, plus an extra excluded-row field.
3. **Deep-control diagnostic weakness, repaired.** The original understated raw/H controls could produce rational strings exceeding the150-character bound and be rejected before arithmetic comparison. The repair uses the upward1e-8 rounding of half the true value. In the independent test, each altered object first passes the entire shape check, then fails with exactly `outward ordered scalar outputs` or `outward panel H`. These are now arithmetic rejection tests. No repair weakened a target.

The original acceptor snapshot and the two actually accepted malformed records are retained under `runs/fixed-target-v7-20261027/acceptor-review01/`. An initial test-harness attempt omitted tuple-assigned constants and failed before validation; that harness mistake is explicitly recorded, not counted as an acceptor result.

## Replay and coverage checks

`accept_entry.py` requires four stages, all24 ordered rho identities at each stage, and exactly128 signed boxes for each stage call. It requires canonical rational strings, exact integer counts excluding booleans, the fixed theorem name and direct pins, and100 adjacent closed probability panels. Prior excluded rows remain explicit. A proposed empty cell must use the exact sentinel; during verification its active/excluded counts must equal the fresh kernel result. \[
R_{\rm recomputed}\le T_{\rm supplied}\le T_{\rm prior},\qquad
D_{\rm recomputed}\le d_{\rm supplied}\le d_{\rm prior}.
\]

The supplied bounds become the next pass's inputs. Therefore the next result is recomputed for those actual bounds, rather than reusing a discovery result computed under different tighter inputs. No stage count, radius, target, branch selector or imported source path is supplied by discovery.

Each last-stage row contributes all100 panels of its fresh actual-cell result. Pooling takes maxima of valid scalar bounds and sums exact incidences. This safely encloses the union of those cells; it does not assert that maxima from different cells are simultaneously attained. `check_panels` requires the full incidence count and an outward bound for every coordinate. Shared panel endpoints remain closed. Empty panels use a fixed sentinel. Consequently no positive-probability panel can disappear through a smaller count or substituted bound. The ancestor's actual-gap inequality and decreasing defect bounds ensure that the retained core exports the actual sign-probability interval at every surviving cell.

The exported theta/rho/beta/H values are coarse joint enclosures for the same law. They do not by themselves discharge a later weighted noise/resource inequality or count comparison. The variance bound theta<1 is recorded; no stronger unspecified legacy noise target is silently claimed.
