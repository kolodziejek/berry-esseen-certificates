\section{Closing the coarse acquisition by keeping the moments together}
\label{v7:signed}
The two initial quadratic passes of the preceding note give an actual
central support gap. The next calculation keeps the signed moments of
one marginal together. It requires no unknown extremal score and uses
only the simple quadratic phase price. The point of the refinement is
not a finer grid: a constraint already present in the conserved defect
can sharply contract a moment box before it is evaluated.

Write $m=\E X_+=\E X_-$, $\beta=\E[X|X|]$, $\gamma=\E X^3$,
$\rho=\E|X|^3$ and $\sigma=\sqrt{10}$. Recall the physical defect
$D=(3+\sigma)\rho-\gamma-3J\ge0$, where $J$ uses the actual support gaps.
The elementary gap inequality $J\le\rho+2m$ and signwise Cauchy give
\begin{equation}\label{v7:coupled}
 D\ge\sigma\rho-\gamma-6m,\qquad
 4m^2+\beta^2\le1.
\end{equation}
Thus, on a box with $\rho\ge l$, $\gamma\le h$ and $D\le d$, put
\[
 m_* =\max\{0,(\sigma_-l-h-d)/6\},\qquad \sigma_-<\sqrt{10}.
\]
If $m_*>1/2$ the box is empty. Otherwise every actual law in it satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{v7:contract}
 m\ge m_*,\qquad |\beta|\le\sqrt{1-4m_*^2}.
\end{equation}
These are simultaneous restrictions, not separately optimized estimates.
They narrow the same beta interval before the sign moments and support
bounds are recomputed. Each gamma slice starts from its original beta
interval; carrying the contracted interval into a different slice would
lose coverage.

A second inexpensive constraint comes from a weighted square. Set
$\delta=\gamma-\rho\beta$ and
$V=\E[|X|^3(|X|-\rho)^2]$. Cauchy with the two factors
$|X|^{3/2}(|X|-\rho)$ and $\operatorname{sgn}(X)|X|^{1/2}$ gives
$\delta^2\le2mV$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{v7:quarter}
 \frac{\rho\E X^4}{2}-\frac{\E|X|^5}{4}
 =\frac{\rho^3}{4}-\frac V4
 \le\frac{\rho^3}{4}-\frac{\delta^2}{8m}.
\end{equation}
Other lower bounds for $V/4$ may be combined by taking their maximum.
They cannot be added: each spends the same nonnegative square.

\subsection{The precise scalar step}
Let $c=C_{\rm E}$, $c_-\le c\le c_+$, and $a\ge0$ be the fixed-target
excess. The raw remainder and nonnegative resource satisfy
\[
 R_c=\mathcal U+n(1-e^{-H^2/(2n)})
          \left(c\rho-\frac{\phi(0)D}{6}\right),\qquad
 \mathcal U=(n+H^2)a+\frac{n\phi(0)D}{6}+n^{3/2}\mathcal L.
\]
Here $H$ is a raw sum threshold, and $\mathcal L\ge0$ is the contact loss.
Suppose the incoming bounds are $R_c\le T<Nc_-l$ and $nD\le Nd$,
with $n\ge N=42$. They give $D\le d$ and the density interval
\[
 \phi(H/\sqrt n)\in[\phi_-,2/5],\qquad
 \phi_-=(997/2500)(1-T/(Nc_-l))>0.
\]
The moment and gap formulas bound the constant charge by $A$ and give
$K_1\in[k_-,k_+]$, $W\in[w_-,w_+]$, where
\[
 K_1=\E[X|X|^3]-3\beta-\tfrac32\rho\gamma,
 \qquad W=J_1+(\E X^4-3)/6.
\]
The preceding Fourier proof supplies $|E_n|\le K_F+s_F|H|$.
For a formal coefficient $v\in[c_-,c_+]$, define
\begin{align*}
 F_v&=\max_{k\in\{k_-,k_+\},\ w\in\{w_-,w_+\},\ \varphi\in\{\phi_-,2/5\}}
                       |vk+\varphi w|+s_F,\\
 Q_v&=vl+\min_{\varphi\in\{\phi_-,2/5\}}\varphi\gamma_-/2,
 \qquad B_v=K_F+vA.
\end{align*}
Require $Q_v>0$ at both endpoints. For $h=|H|$ the actual raw remainder
is bounded by $B_c+F_ch-Q_ch^2$. For each fixed $h$, this upper bound is
convex in $c$: $F_c$ is a maximum of absolute affine functions, and the
other terms are affine. Therefore it is bounded by the maximum of the
two endpoint quadratics, even when $A$ is negative.
Set
\[
 T'=\min\left\{T,\max_{v=c_-,c_+}
                    \left(B_v+\frac{F_v^2}{4Q_v}\right)\right\}.
\]
A negative $T'$ excludes the box, since $R_c\ge0$. Otherwise the previous
strict raw gate remains valid. The simple phase lemma gives
\[
 \eta=\tfrac12(c_-l-T'/N)>0,\qquad
 \mathcal U\le\max_{v=c_-,c_+}
                    \{B_v+F_vh-(Q_v+\eta)h^2\}.
\]
A negative maximum of these quadratics excludes the box. In every other
box put
\[
 S=\max_{v=c_-,c_+}\left(B_v+\frac{F_v^2}{4(Q_v+\eta)}\right),
 \qquad d'=\min\{d,6S/(N(997/2500))\}.
\]
Then $nD\le Nd'$. In particular this is a weighted bound for every
$n\ge42$, not just an estimate evaluated at $n=42$. A valid phase ceiling is
\[
 |H|\le\max_{v=c_-,c_+}
 \frac{F_v+\sqrt{F_v^2+4(Q_v+\eta)\max(0,B_v)}}{2(Q_v+\eta)}.
\]
The replacement of $B_v$ by its nonnegative part only enlarges this root.
All uses of the improved resource follow the raw-license check; there is
no circular use of an unlicensed density floor.

\subsection{Geometry, support and complete coverage}
The actual-gap identity also improves the high-frequency event.

consequences
\[
 m(A_0^2+B_0^2)\ge l-\frac d{\sigma_- -1},\qquad
 6mA_0B_0\ge(\sigma_- -1)l-d

\[
 (A_0+B_0)^2\ge
 \frac{l-d/(\sigma_- -1)+\{(\sigma_- -1)l-d\}/3}{m_u}.
\]
The event is the actual opposite-sign pair, not a substituted two-point
law. Its probability and conditional distance moment retain their
