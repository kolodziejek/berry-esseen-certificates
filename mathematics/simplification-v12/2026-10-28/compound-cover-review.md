## Independent coverage argument

The 291 boxes are contained in the exact closed rectangle

`lambda in [3/100,3/40], r in [0,3/20], w in [0,1]`.

I checked every pair for intersection of all three open interiors, and
there is none. The sum of the exact rational volumes is `27/4000`, equal
to the target volume. This establishes full coverage, including faces:
the finite union of closed boxes is closed; an uncovered point of the
target would have a relatively open neighborhood of positive target
volume, contradicting volume equality. This is independent of the
constructor's common endpoint refinement in `compound-accept.py:30`.
For comparison, that implementation accepts the same document and reports
480 elementary refinement boxes. There are 200 quartic and 91 Cantelli
cells. Closed boundaries are deliberately shared; interior overlap is
rejected.

Independent mutations remove a cell, duplicate a cell, shrink a cell by
half, or replace a missing cell by a duplicate. The volume or intersection
test rejects each. Constructor validation additionally rejects reordered
cells, changed domain, a hinge at its threshold, Boolean later anchors,
noncanonical rational strings and missing fields. Raw object keys are
unique in the pinned JSON, checked with a duplicate-key-rejecting parser.
This review does not claim that the constructor's ordinary `json.loads`
rejects every possible malformed raw JSON encoding; its listed schema

## Continuous reduction and arithmetic semantics

The own-count physical multipliers remain `g0,g1`, with the allocation
`w=p v1/s`. In `compound-accept.py:122`, the substitutions
`g_j=1+sign_j*(5/2)*(Xi+r)*eta` are made before the polynomial is
expanded. The code therefore preserves the linear dependence on phase,
including all terms generated by `-3(T0+T1)^2`. It does not identify
physical allocation with the own-count variance fraction.

For fixed other variables the proof's expression is separately concave in
`g0,g1`, then in `lambda,w,eta` after the displayed substitutions, and
affine in `m`. The quadratic terms have the required nonpositive signs;
the marginal cubic term contributes `-K0*(13/16)*lambda^2*eta^2`.
Thus the 256 endpoint combinations in each quartic cell cover the actual
interior of these intervals. The artificial face `eta=0` completes a
closed concavity interval and does not assert a limiting theorem about a
varying law. The remaining variable `r` is a degree-four polynomial.

I reconstructed its transform by solving the five-by-five rational
interpolation problem for each monomial `r^k`, `k=0,...,4`, at five
distinct normalized phase points. The resulting coefficients equal the
constructor's matrix in `compound-accept.py:99` on all 12 phase intervals.
Three additional non-interpolation points verify each reconstruction.
This independently verifies all 300 transform entries, rather than
restating the constructor's combinatorial formula. Positive Bernstein
coefficients include both interval endpoints.

The two later-count denominators are checked **before squaring** for all
291 cells. The first incoming hinge has `0<=y<1+r_lower` on each complete
cell. The fixed infinite-tail denominator and ratio have the correct
positive direction; adding nonexistent legal counts only enlarges the
upper bound. The centered compound evaluator retains the complete
Poisson tail through the finite lower-complement identity, rather than
discarding a numerical tail. Its deeper comparison and evaluation review
remains a separate component.

For all 91 Cantelli cells I independently checked the initial derivative
license using a slightly looser rational exponential upper bound. This
also licenses the constructor's sharper bound. The derivative first
increases and then decreases, so a positive initial derivative permits
only an interior maximum; the minimum is at an endpoint. The code chooses
`m_lower` or `m_upper` according to the sign of the entire bracket and
checks the sign needed to replace `lambda*eta` by its upper bound.

One complete quartic cell was reproduced. Replacing its positive scale
by `alpha=.01` produces a negative certified margin, demonstrating that
an arithmetically false parameter is rejected beyond schema checks.
A later hinge `j=99` rejects before division. A disposable copy with one
newline appended to the evaluator is rejected at its byte hash before
the cell loop. No constructor file was changed for these controls.

## Scope and unresolved boundaries

The accepted certificate covers the signed comparison only when
`n>=42`, `p in [.4,.53]`, amplitude `.6`, inward sum `<=.072`, raw
`|H|<=1.84`, target `C>=.4`, `lambda in [.03,.075]`, and phase
`u=-r in [-.15,0]`. The proof does not acquire these hypotheses.
It covers zero conditional variance through `w=0,1`; it does not cover
zero total noise, since the noise interval begins at `.03`.
Strict outgoing and closed incoming events retain threshold atoms.

No new computation is needed at the closed join `lambda=.03`, but the
smaller-noise theorem's actual target and strip hypotheses must still be
matched. Other phases, reflected acquisition, probabilities below `.4`,
larger noise, and the finite base through 41 remain separate obligations.
Within this exact domain, replacing a count-curvature or half-amplitude
exclusion is justified only after the separate phase joins are supplied.
The certificate alone does not justify deleting their uses elsewhere or
asserting the full sharp Berry--Esseen conjecture.
