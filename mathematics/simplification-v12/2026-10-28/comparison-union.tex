The count comparison now needs only an own-count moment estimate and an
incoming-tail estimate. The same conditional third moments enter both the
own-count loss and the marginal penalty; pricing them together avoids paying
twice for incompatible worst cases. On the right, the first incoming count
also retains the two different jump bounds. All later counts have one common
variance bound, which gives a complete factorial tail. This is the selected
route for further simplification.

\begin{theorem}[Union of the reviewed component domains]\label{thm:union}
Let $n\ge42$, $Y=B+U$ with $B$ Bernoulli of parameter $p$ and
$\E(U\mid B)=0$. Put $v=p(1-p)$, $s=\E U^2>0$, $\lambda=ns$,
$X=(Y-p)/\sqrt{v+s}$, $\rho=\E|X|^3$, and
$\rho_B=(1-2v)/\sqrt v$. Assume $|H|\le46/25$.
Let $S_n$ be the sum of independent copies of $X$ and $F_B$ the
$\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ CDF. Each row below supplies an integer $k$ such that,
with $\delta=k-np$,
\begin{align*}
 \Delta_C={}&\sqrt n\{\Pp(S_n\le H)-\Phi(H/\sqrt n)\}-C\rho\\
 &-\sqrt n\{F_B(k)-\Phi(\delta/\sqrt{nv})\}+C\rho_B<0.
\end{align*}
The support convention in the table is
$-A\le U_0\le a_0$, $-a_1\le U_1\le A$, with $a_j\ge0$.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
 p&A&a_0+a_1\le&0<\lambda\le&C\\\hline
 [1/4,2/5]&7/10&17/250&1/100&[819/2000,41/100]\\
 {[2/5,53/100]}&3/5&9/125&3/40&[2/5,\infty)
\end{array}
\]
The left row has the stronger conclusion $\Delta_C<-\lambda/100$.
The right row asserts only strict negativity on its enlarged domain.
The previously established right theorem with $\lambda\le.03$ retains
its stronger margin, and the older $\lambda\le.02$ theorem for all
$C\ge0$ is unchanged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In the left row choose the phase $u=np+H\sqrt{v+s}-k$ in
$[-.218,.782]$ and apply LEFT-01 in \texttt{leftcount.tex}.
In the right row choose $u\in[-.272,.728]$. Both intervals have
width one, so such anchors exist. If $\lambda\le.03$, the existing
theorem in \texttt{counts-v11.tex} applies. For $.03\le\lambda\le.075$,
the negative inner phase $u\in[-.15,0]$ is covered by
\texttt{compound-proof.tex}; the other phases are covered by
\texttt{outer.tex}. Endpoints are included in both adjoining intervals,
and no phase or noise gap remains. Each component uses the same actual
conditional laws, and each retains strict outgoing and closed incoming
events. No optimizer of a moment relaxation is substituted for an iid law.
\end{proof}

\section*{How this joins to the acquired witness}
For an original standardized witness $Z$, let $B$ label its two conditional
parts and $m_j=\E(Z\mid B=j)$, with separation $\ell=m_1-m_0>0$.
Then $m_0=-p\ell$, $m_1=(1-p)\ell$. Put
\[
 U=(Z-m_B)/\ell,\qquad Y=B+U=(Z-m_0)/\ell.
\]
These are the actual marginal conditional variables. If
$\theta=\E(Z-m_B)^2$, then
\[
 s=\theta/\ell^2,\qquad v+s=1/\ell^2,\qquad
 (Y-p)/\sqrt{v+s}=Z,\qquad\lambda=n\theta/\ell^2.
\]
Thus the raw sum threshold $H$ and the original law are preserved.
The v11 left support rays imply the left amplitude and inward assumptions;
the v10/v11 right strips imply the right ones. Those acquisition results
retain their contact, heat, actual-index and sharp-predecessor hypotheses.
The direct comparison itself has none of those premises, but it cannot
supply its own strip or threshold bounds.

At the sharp Bernoulli constant
$C_E=(3+\sqrt{10})/(6\sqrt{2\pi})$, both coefficient restrictions hold.
The external strict finite-$n$ Bernoulli theorem bounds the bracket at
every integer anchor. Hence a witness of nonnegative excess satisfying
either row is excluded. At zero noise the law is itself Bernoulli and that
external theorem applies directly. The negative signed discrepancy still
requires reflection and the appropriate attained or one-sided CDF witness;
this corollary does not bypass those steps.

\section*{Exact dependency changes}
Within the two rows, once their premises have been acquired, no additional
half-amplitude confinement, harmonic or fractional-part gain, marked
crossings, count-curvature estimate or inherited full-count grid is needed to close the
count comparison. Each replacement is explicit:
\begin{itemize}
 \item On the left, the joint estimate in \texttt{leftcount.tex} supplies
 an additional $7s/4$ reserve through guarded endpoint prices.
 Separately discarding the unfavorable
 left moment correction would be false.
 \item The right negative inner count uses physical multipliers
 $1-\delta/(nq)$ and $1+\delta/(np)$. Its first incoming probability
 is bounded by a centered two-jump Poisson hinge. A finite complement
 evaluates that hinge with its whole tail included. Its 291 closed
 rational boxes cover noise, phase and variance allocation; sample size
 is handled by concavity in $1/n$, not by a finite list of sample sizes.
 \item The other right phases use the bound
 $\operatorname{Var}W_j\le(5/2)\lambda$ for every count, followed by
 a factorial continuation. Two polynomials on one rectangle and five
 scalar endpoint comparisons complete those phases.
\end{itemize}
The left proof has 88 scalar checks. The right negative-inner acceptance
has 23 normalization checks, 200 quartic cells and 91 Cantelli cells;
each quartic cell has 256 endpoint choices and five Bernstein coefficients.
The other right phases have 56 scalar checks and 144 coefficients.
These are bounded executable inequalities, not a certificate-free proof.
The external quadratic-hinge comparison remains an analytic dependency.

\section*{What is still missing}
The acquired left range above $\lambda=.01$ and right range above
$\lambda=.075$ remain open up to their retained panel-specific budgets.
The direct geometry's global bound $\lambda<.616$ is much larger than
either closed range. There is no global propagation theorem here.
An independent finite base through 41 remains missing, and the retained
11,264-cell coarse Fourier entry has not been replaced. These prevent a
claim of the full sharp conjecture.

Original sources and the manuscript are preserved. The two false shortcuts
are documented in \texttt{compound-review.tex}; neither is used here.
Source-bound reviews and run receipts are supplied separately. Exact
arithmetic does not replace acquisition, attainment or external theorems.
