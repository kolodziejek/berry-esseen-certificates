\title{Toward a shorter proof of the sharp iid Berry--Esseen bound:\\
fixed-target excess and a common noise measure}
\author{Bartosz Ko{\l}odziejek \and Kacper Rodziewicz}
\date{27 October 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Subtracting the proposed Berry--Esseen bound before maximizing fixes
the coefficient in the contact equation. We prove attainment for this
excess problem, derive its zero-bias identity, and state exact conditions
under which existing scalar estimates transfer to the new extremizer.
Under those conditions, a first-failure argument needs no upper-excess
entry bound. We also give a short proof of a sharp family of gap inequalities
and retain a common positive measure when bounding conditional noise.
These are analytic reductions. A complete finite base and uniform local
exclusion remain to be established; this note does not prove the conjecture.
\end{abstract}
