## Accepted mathematical interfaces

asymmetry charge from the same physical defect before spending its remainder
on noise and displacement. Its third-moment turnover is the maximum of a
piecewise affine upper bound, not the sum of incompatible optimizations.
Multiplying the original inequality by the actual n preserves the weighted

**Signed phase.** The previous pass licenses the density floor. The resulting
raw ceiling is proved before it licenses the extra positive quadratic phase
price. Signed slopes can be negative. Convexity in the fixed coefficient c
justifies its two endpoints, and concavity in |H| requires retaining an interior
vertex. Every active cell and every closed panel overlap is kept. The result
bounds the same joint excess/defect/leakage resource used by confinement.

**Continuous low-noise replacement.** The new uniform argument needs only
theta<.0074, ell>1.992 and rho<1.105, derived from lambda<=.075 and the incoming
`.6` strip. Set z=p for the negative physical ray and z=1−p for the positive

baseline entry was removed. Every proposed replacement is limited to the
domain proved in the README. The failed fixed-resource, redistributed-grid,
