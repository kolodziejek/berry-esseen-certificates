\noindent
We prove a comparison on an explicit small-noise domain, uniform over all
integers \(n\ge42\). This is one part of a possible proof of the sharp iid
Berry--Esseen bound. It does not establish the full bound.
Throughout, \(S_n=X_1+\cdots+X_n\) for independent copies of the indicated
standardized law \(X\), \(\Phi\) is the standard normal distribution
function, and
\(\CE=(3+\sqrt{10})/(6\sqrt{2\pi})\).
The function \(F_B\) is the distribution function of \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)\).
The final implication uses the strict finite-\(n\) Bernoulli theorem of
Schulz, \emph{The optimal Berry--Esseen constant in the binomial case},
dissertation, Theorem~1, p.~15,
\href{https://d-nb.info/1197702695/34}{German National Library copy}.
