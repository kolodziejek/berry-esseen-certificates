## Independent check of the scalar entry

Let `c=C_E<41/100`, `a_m=C_m-c`, and use the independently derived support-free recurrence

\[
C_m\le s C_{m-1}+\frac{\sqrt m}{15(m-1)^{3/2}}
                         +\frac{2\sqrt m}{25(m-1)^2},
\qquad s=\sqrt{m/(m-1)}.
\]

For `m>=40` and `C_(m-1)<=c<C_m`,

\[
0<ma_m\le\frac m{m-1}
\left\{\frac c{s+1}+\frac s{15}+\frac{2s}{25\sqrt{m-1}}\right\}.
\]

The elementary endpoint inequalities are

\[
\frac m{m-1}\le\frac{40}{39},\quad 1<s<\frac{1013}{1000},\quad
\sqrt{m-1}>6,
\quad39(1013)^2-40(1000)^2=20591>0.
\]

Thus

\[
ma_m<\frac{40}{39}
\left(\frac{41}{200}+\frac{1013}{15000}+\frac{1013}{75000}\right)
=\frac{7151}{24375}<\frac3{10}<.54.
\]

The exact reserve below `.3` is `323/48750`. Hence `C_m<c+.3/m<.41+.3/40=167/400=.4175<.4226<.4247`; the reserve below `.4226` is `51/10000`. Zero decrement follows directly from `C_(m-1)<=c<C_m`; both deleted caps follow from their sharp bounds and `c<.41<.4253`.

This supplies the A17 scalar interface by direct inequalities and uses no old positive ceiling. It does **not** prove a local exclusion at arbitrary `m>=40`: the local package under review is explicitly finite40–42, and its signed inverse and favorable heat terms require that finite domain. No new scalar script was run; this small exact derivation is given in full for review.
