all-law theorem graph. Original final joins additionally match actual acquired
rows to local targets and enforce full probability, signed phase and noise
coverage. For example F`check_cutoff44_composition.py:51–88` binds the same

joint quadratics, then all noise profiles up to *actual* Lambda. Its
`comparisons` cover logic is at lines17–49. These obligations survive a change
to the last global scalar argument.

The current `42..48` finite range enters support and acquisition through
literal `N=42,L=48`, e.g. that collector's lines51–56, and the support caller
above. Its 78/74 LOW rows and the 45/47 probability panels are reused across
this range. Changing `L=48` to46 would be a new theorem/certificate interface,
not permission to drop arbitrary rows with numerical labels47 or48. A shared
program may also remain needed even if one of its parameterized targets is
